Evidence & Retention Policy
HIPAA 45 CFR 164.530(j)
Chain of Custody
Every artifact is SHA-256 hashed at collection time. Hash values are stored in a chain-of-custody JSON alongside the forensic ZIP archive, ensuring tamper-evident evidence for regulatory examination.
Retention Schedule
Incident records and evidence packages are retained for a minimum of 6 years per HIPAA 45 CFR 164.530(j). Each archive is tagged with retention metadata at creation.
Artifact Inventory
Each evidence package captures: raw input, detection results, containment logs, notification receipts, system metadata, and timestamp records — all in a single auditable ZIP archive.
